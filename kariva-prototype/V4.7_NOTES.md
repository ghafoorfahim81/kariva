# Kariva V4.7 — UX & Profile Redesign (prototype)

Changes: full-width discovery result cards with short bios and stars only; inline review creation on public person/business profiles; favorite profiles with browser-local persistence; owner bio near avatar; app slogans on Home instead of private profile; language and identity selectors in Settings; sample feed comments; demo login/register/reset screens accessible from Home; explicit page management shortcut from Settings.

Limitations: authentication is a UI mock, not real login or registration. Reviews and comments are local in-memory demo state. Favorites are browser-local. English/Pashto translation is partial: many legacy demo strings remain in Dari. No server, real identity verification, or real access controls. Build and iPhone testing were not verified in this environment. Do not enter real credentials or identity documents.

Business page creation: Profile > Business pages, or Settings > Manage and create business pages.
